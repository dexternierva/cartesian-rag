Writing

The words are part of the system.

Rule

Shopper words, not engine words

“Found 2 chairs that match everything you asked for”, not “2 rows pass every rule”.

Rule

Say what a number is about

“48 navy AeroTask Pro Mesh available at Indianapolis Regional”, not “Available: 48”.

Rule

Buttons say verb, object, consequence

“Hold 40 chairs for 15 min”, never “Submit” or wording that sounds like a charge.

Rule

Say no with a way out

“Nothing matches all of that right now”, then a button for the one thing to let go of.

Rule

Be honest about time

“Usually 6 to 40 seconds”, with the real steps.

Rule

Be honest about the source

“Real answers, recorded 19 Nov 2026” in a demo; “live” only when live.
