# HoldNotice

The two steps after pressing Hold: **Confirm** (`lantern-soft` with a `lantern` edge) and **Held** (`forest-soft`, lock icon, countdown in `timer`).

- Confirm repeats exactly what will be held and for how much, and says it costs nothing yet.
- Held says what happens when the countdown reaches zero, so the timer informs rather than alarms.
- The countdown is `ink`, never `campfire`.
